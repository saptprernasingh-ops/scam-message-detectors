    How It Works

    1

    Learn

    The model reads 4,459 labelled messages and counts how often each word appears in spam and in normal messages.

    2

    Split

    Your message is broken into words, e.g. "WIN a FREE prize" becomes win, a, free, prize.

    3

    Calculate

    Using the Naive Bayes formula, it works out how likely those words are in spam versus normal messages.

    4

    Decide

    If the spam chance is 50% or more, the message is flagged and the most spam-like words are highlighted.

    How to Spot a Scam Message

    ⏰ Urgency

    "Account blocked today", "offer expires in 1 hour". Scammers rush you so you don't think.

    🎁 Too good to be true

    Prizes, lotteries and jobs you never applied for.

    🔗 Unknown links

    Don't click links in messages. Open the official app or website yourself.

    🔐 Asking for OTP / PIN

    Banks never ask for your OTP, PIN or password by message or call.

    💸 Asking for payment

    "Pay a small fee to receive your prize" is always a scam.

    📞 Report it

    In India, report fraud at cybercrime.gov.in or call 1930.
